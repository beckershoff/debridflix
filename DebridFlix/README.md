# DebridFlix (Enigma2 / OpenATV 7.6)

Netflix-style browser for your receiver: browse TMDB, find streams via Orion or Torrentio, see
what is cached on your debrid service (Premiumize, Real-Debrid, TorBox, AllDebrid, Debrid-Link)
and play it over HTTPS in the box's own player. English and German interface; English audio
preferred, German second; subtitles German first, then English.

First start shows a QR code: scan it with your phone (same network) to enter your keys.
Settings: `/etc/enigma2/debridflix.json`. Debug log (Setup > Debug log): `/tmp/debridflix.log`.

Full documentation: https://github.com/beckershoff/debridflix

Use it only for content you are allowed to stream.
